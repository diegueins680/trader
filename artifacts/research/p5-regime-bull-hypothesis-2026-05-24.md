## 1. Hypothesis (H₁)

On a **trending-up cryptocurrency dataset** (≥5,000 bars, 4h or 5m granularity), the long-biased signal generated by `--method 01 --epochs 0` with vol-targeting will achieve:

- **Sharpe ratio ≥ 0.20**
- **Closed trades ≥ 50**
- **Max drawdown ≤ 15%**

**Null hypothesis (H₀):** Even in a rising market, the signal remains unprofitable (Sharpe < 0.20).

## 2. Why This Matters

P3 and P4 showed catastrophic performance on BNBUSDT 5m June 2020 (strong downtrend). The strategy is structurally long-biased (LSTM-bypass with `--epochs 0` produces an upward-skewed signal). A rising market is the **minimum viable regime** for this signal. If it fails here, the method should be deprecated.
