# Paper-to-repository gap matrix

| Literature method | Repository implementation | Differing assumptions | Deliberate? | Likely economic importance | Feasible / justified action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| ARCH/GARCH | Rolling volatility, EWMA-like controls, Kalman noise; no fitted GARCH sensor | Heuristic/rolling risk estimates instead of likelihood volatility | Yes | Medium for calibration and risk sizing | Compare GARCH/EWMA/HAR offline; simplest indistinguishable model wins |
| Realized volatility / HAR-RV | Bar-return volatility features and volatility targeting | No high-frequency realized-variance aggregation; no explicit daily/weekly/monthly HAR regression | No documented fidelity claim | High for uncertainty/risk, low for direction | Prospectively prototype a bar-HAR risk gate; label bar-squared-return deviation explicitly |

## Gap priorities

1. **Safety/correctness:** explicit missingness and availability in the predictor schema; safe abstention on required-feature failure.
2. **Semantic compatibility:** accurate implementation identities for the three sequence proxies while preserving legacy IDs.
3. **Research validity:** keep formal DSR/PBO and complete trial accounting at the promotion boundary; never substitute production proxies.
4. **Economic fidelity:** improve fill/impact/liquidation replay before considering OFI or any higher-turnover candidate.
5. **Provenance:** versioned model artifacts before any new persisted challenger.
6. **Architecture:** only after the preceding gaps and only with incremental net evidence. A faithful Transformer is not a priority by itself.
