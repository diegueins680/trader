## Assumptions and refinement limits

A-PROMOTION-BOUNDARY and the existing component assumptions explicitly trust pinned
language/compiler/library and primitive-effect semantics, ordinary numeric/built-in
objects, registered scalar identifiers, fixed runner/trainer code and installed helper
identity. No hostile subclasses, monkeypatching, replaced binaries/PATH or manual
artifact conversion. Exclusive-create/fresh-directory semantics and stable path
namespace are assumed; hostile concurrent filesystem mutation is outside scope.

The inventory is drift detection and exhaustive finite source coverage, not proof
of Python semantics. Correspondence of reviewed source operations to abstract
primitive effects is an explicit assumption; actual predicates are translated to
SMT and actual codecs/writers exercise their boundaries. Existing Haskell type,
negative-compilation and model-derived protocol conformance remain required.
This is conditional compositional evidence, not whole-language or compiler refinement.

No inherited production authorization, all-paper/shadow authority, deployed-image,
cryptographic provenance truth, persistent ownership, whole-champion filesystem
isolation, crash atomicity or OS sandbox claim. In particular 13/28/29/30/33/35/37
do not close from this result. Any new callback, consumer, write destination, format,
